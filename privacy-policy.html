IS-Assist Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

IS-Assist is an AI-powered procurement assistant designed to analyze procurement specifications on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM) and provide applicable Indian Standards and relevant product recommendations.

1. Information We Access

When the user explicitly activates the IS-Assist extension on a supported GeM webpage, the extension may access the content of the current webpage that is necessary to analyze the procurement or tender specification.

This may include product specifications, technical parameters, requirements, and other tender-related webpage content.

2. How We Use Information

The accessed webpage content is used solely to provide the core functionality of IS-Assist, including extracting specifications, identifying applicable Indian Standards, and generating relevant product recommendations.

3. Data Transmission

Relevant tender and specification content may be transmitted securely to the IS-Assist backend service for processing and analysis.

The backend processes the submitted information to generate the requested recommendations and analysis.

4. Personal Information

IS-Assist does not intentionally request or collect personal information such as passwords, payment information, health information, or personal communications as part of its core functionality.

Users should avoid submitting unnecessary personal information through tender or specification content.

5. Data Sharing

IS-Assist does not sell user data or use user data for advertising, creditworthiness decisions, lending purposes, or unrelated commercial purposes.

Data is processed only as necessary to provide the functionality of the IS-Assist extension.

6. Data Security

Data transmitted between the extension and the IS-Assist backend is transmitted using HTTPS where supported.

7. User Control

Users control when the extension is activated and when webpage content is submitted for analysis. Users may stop using or uninstall the extension at any time.

8. Third-Party Services

IS-Assist may use third-party infrastructure or AI services to process requests necessary to provide its functionality. Such services are used only for processing required to provide the requested analysis.

9. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the functionality or data handling practices of IS-Assist change. The latest version will be made available at this URL.
